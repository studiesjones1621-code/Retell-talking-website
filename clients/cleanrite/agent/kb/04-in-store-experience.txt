CLEAN RITE CENTER — WHAT THE STORES ARE LIKE

Clean Rite calls itself a laundromat superstore, and the difference from a
corner laundromat is size. 150+ machines means you are not waiting for a washer,
and you can run a week of laundry at once instead of over three loads.

AMENITIES
Free WiFi
Large screen cable TVs
Free parking
Vending machines with snacks and drinks
Comfortable lounge seating
Ample laundry carts and folding tables
Detergent and supplies for purchase
Friendly attendants on the floor to help

MACHINES
Washers and dryers from 20 lb to 80 lb. Faster spin cycles leave fabrics with
less moisture so drying takes less time. Dryer cycles are sensor-based rather
than timed, so loads come out actually dry.

PAYMENT
Machines are card operated, not coin. The laundry card earns rewards just for
being a customer. Clean Rite does not take payment over the phone or through
this assistant.

WHY PEOPLE CHOOSE IT
In business since 2000, which is 25 years. Over 20,000 five-star reviews
nationwide. Spacious, clean, safe and well-lit, which matters a lot to people
doing laundry late at night.
