HOW TO ANSWER "WHERE ARE YOU"
Ask which area they are in, then name the borough or city count. For Baltimore
callers you can give the two addresses directly — there are only two, so it is
more useful than sending them to a locator.

For New York, do not recite addresses. There are forty. Ask for their
neighborhood and point them at the store locator on cleanritecenter.com, which
sorts by distance.

If someone asks about a state not on this list, say Clean Rite is in New York,
Maryland and Pennsylvania right now, and offer the locator in case a new store
has opened since.
