CLEAN RITE CENTER — SERVICES AND PRICING

WASH, DRY & FOLD (drop off)
Bring in a bag of laundry. We wash it, dry it, fold it, and have it ready when
you come back. No sorting or appointment needed.

PICKUP & DELIVERY
We collect from your door and bring it back folded. Free pickup and delivery
within 3 miles of a store. Available up to 10 miles; past 3 miles a delivery
fee may apply. Driving is handled by Uber and DoorDash partners. You get a text
at every step: picked up, weighed, on the way back.

SELF SERVICE
Every store has 150+ washers and dryers, from 20 lb up to 80 lb. Carts, folding
tables, detergent and supplies for sale, and attendants on the floor. Machines
are card operated and the card earns rewards.

COMFORTERS AND BULKY ITEMS
The 80 lb machines take comforters, duvets, blankets and bedding that will not
fit a home washer. This is one of the most common reasons people come in.

PRICING FOR WASH-DRY-FOLD AND DELIVERY
$29.99 for the first 15 pounds.
$2.19 for each additional pound.
Say this aloud as "twenty-nine ninety-nine for the first fifteen pounds, then
two nineteen a pound after that."

The customer reviews and confirms the total after the laundry is weighed at the
store. Nothing is charged before they confirm. That confirmation step is the
answer to "how do I know I won't get overcharged."

WHAT WE CANNOT QUOTE
A total for a specific load, because it depends on weight. Give the per-pound
rate and explain the confirm-before-charge step instead. A typical household
load is usually somewhere around 15 to 25 pounds, but do not present that as a
quote.
