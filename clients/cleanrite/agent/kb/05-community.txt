CLEAN RITE CENTER — COMMUNITY WORK

Only bring this up if the caller asks about free wash events, community
programs, benefits help, or why Clean Rite partners with nonprofits. It is not a
selling point to lead with.

FABRIC HEALTH PARTNERSHIP
Clean Rite works with Fabric Health to run FREE WASH events and to help
customers sign up for Medicaid and SNAP benefits while they wait for their
laundry. The idea is that most customers spend around two hours a week in the
store, so it is a practical place to reach people who would otherwise have to
navigate that paperwork alone.

DARNELL CARES
A partnership supporting NYC communities through clean clothing initiatives.

FREE WASH EVENTS
These run periodically and are announced on the events page of the website and
on Facebook and Instagram. Do not promise a date. Point people at the events
page at cleanritecenter.com.

IMPORTANT
Do not give benefits advice, do not check eligibility, and do not collect any
health or benefits information. Point them to the events page or to Fabric
Health at the event itself.
