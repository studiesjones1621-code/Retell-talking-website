CLEAN RITE CENTER — TURNAROUND TIMES

Three options:
- 4-hour rapid clean
- 24 hour
- 2 day

The 4-hour rapid clean is the one people react to. Very few laundromats in New
York offer a 4-hour wash-dry-fold turnaround, and customers mention it in
reviews unprompted. If somebody is in a hurry, lead with it.

WHAT AFFECTS TIMING
Rapid turnaround depends on the store and how busy it is. Do not promise a
specific finish time for a specific store. Say which turnaround options exist
and let the store confirm on drop-off.

HOURS
Hours vary by location. Many stores are open 24 hours, which is why people use
Clean Rite for overnight shifts and late-night catch-up. Do not state a specific
store's hours from memory — send them to the store locator on the website. The
hours listed in some directories are wrong.
