# What can be configured

Everything below is adjustable per client. These answer "can it also…" questions.
Do not recite this list unprompted — answer the capability actually asked about.

## Spam and robocall filtering
Yes. Known spam patterns and robocalls can be filtered so they never reach the
client or their team, and never generate a notification. This is a real running
cost for busy lines and is worth confirming when someone complains about junk
calls.

## Appointment reminders
Yes. Automatic reminders before the appointment, by text or call, on the
client's schedule. This is usually the single cheapest way to cut no-shows.

## Review requests after the appointment
Yes. Once the appointment window has passed, the customer can be invited to
leave a review, routed to whichever platform the client wants — Google Business
Profile most often. It runs automatically off the booking, so nobody on staff
has to remember.

## Live transfer to the right person
Yes. The agent is trained on the client's roster and who covers what, so it
transfers to the correct person rather than a general line. Warm transfer — it
briefs whoever picks up.

## Booking, rescheduling and cancelling
Yes, all three, against the client's real calendar availability.

## Completed intake handed to staff
The agent works through the client's intake questions during the call and routes
a finished intake to the person who needs it, so the callback starts informed
rather than from a name and number.

## After-hours only / overflow only
Yes. It can take every call, only the ones after closing, or only the ones the
team cannot pick up.

## Multiple locations or providers
Yes — routing by location, provider or service is configured per client.

## Does it integrate with [specific software]?
Do NOT promise a named integration. The honest answer: "It books against your
calendar. For [their software] specifically, that's a setup question — tell the
team which system you're on and they'll confirm exactly how it connects on the
call." Never claim compatibility you have not confirmed.

## What it will never do
- Collect payment details, card numbers or SSNs
- Give medical, legal or clinical advice
- Quote a price the client has not approved
- Promise a response time the client has not committed to
