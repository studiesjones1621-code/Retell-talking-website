# Dental practices — real questions, real answers

## Is it HIPAA compliant?
A Business Associate Agreement is in place with the voice infrastructure the
agent runs on, and OnDuty Agent signs a BAA with the practice as well. The
practice controls what is stored — everything, PII excluded, or basic call
attributes only — and retention from one day to two years. Recording access is
through signed, expiring links.
Do NOT say "we are HIPAA compliant" as a flat badge. Say what is actually in
place, which is more convincing to the person who evaluates this.

## Does it work with Dentrix / Eaglesoft / Open Dental / Curve?
It books against the schedule. For a named practice-management system, ask which
one they run and say the team confirms exactly how it connects on the call. Do
not claim a specific integration before it is confirmed.

## Can it verify insurance?
It captures carrier and plan during the call so the front desk has it. Actual
eligibility verification is a setup question — take it to the demo rather than
promising it.

## What happens with a dental emergency after hours?
It follows the practice's own on-call protocol: recognise the emergency, reach
the on-call clinician, or give the practice's instructions. It does not leave a
message nobody hears until Monday.

## Will it handle reschedules and cancellations?
Yes, and it offers the slot a cancellation just opened to the next caller, which
is where most of the recovered revenue comes from.

## What about recall and reactivation calls?
Yes — the practice sets who gets contacted and when.

## Can it answer treatment or clinical questions?
No. Anything clinical routes to a person. It handles scheduling, insurance
capture at the administrative level, hours, location and new-patient intake.

## Our front desk is already good. Why would we need this?
Not a replacement question — a coverage one. The calls it catches are the ones
ringing while the team is chairside, the ones after closing, and the second line
when the first is busy. Ask what happens to a call at 5:40pm on a Friday right
now.

## What does the front desk actually get?
A completed new-patient record from the call — contact, carrier and plan, reason
for the visit — instead of a name on a message pad.

## Reminders and reviews?
Yes. Appointment reminders before the visit, and an automatic review request
once the appointment window passes, routed to Google Business Profile.

## Can it block spam calls?
Yes — filtered before they reach the practice.
